# Founding governance proposal

This is a proposal, not a finalized constitution. Maintainers review contributions through public pull requests and document technical decisions. Keep admin access limited, require least-privilege credentials and retain reproducible checkpoint history. Contributors receive credit through public history; participation is voluntary with no employment or compensation promise.

Treat contributors respectfully. No harassment, discrimination, unsolicited private contact, requests for personal information, or publication of private messages. Keep discussions in moderated project channels. Report concerns using GitHub's or Discord's reporting tools or to a designated moderator. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. Moderators may remove harmful content and restrict accounts.

Before accepting public training updates, agree on artifact limits, validation, quarantine, rollback and abuse handling. Licensing and maintainer roles require explicit agreement.
